Cassie Video Uploader

Developed with YouTube API Services

Cassie Video Uploader is a personal, single-user command-line tool. It uploads educational videos that I (Yue Liang) produce myself — the nudgecode 算法图解 algorithm tutorial series — to my own YouTube channel: youtube.com/channel/UC5sy1-gDL7FFqQ40Xh80saw.

What it does

  • Signs in once with Google OAuth 2.0 (scope: upload to my own channel).
  • Uploads a rendered MP4 with its title, description, tags, thumbnail and playlist (videos.insert, thumbnails.set, playlistItems.insert).
  • Runs only on my own computer. There are no other users, no accounts, and no public web service.

Why

The series has 40+ episodes (each 4–8 minutes). Uploading them by hand through the web interface is slow and error-prone; the tool uploads them in order with consistent metadata.
